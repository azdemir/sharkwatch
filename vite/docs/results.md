**How to read this:**
- **Sharks confirmed** are tracks Muse Spark verified at ≥ 0.75 confidence. Tracks are per appearance, so an animal that leaves the frame and comes back counts twice.
- **Ruled out** are SAM candidates Muse Spark rejected (rays, turtles and so on), so they never raised an alert.
- **Uncertain** go to a human instead of alerting.
- **Untrusted lengths** are sharks whose SAM outline merges a neighbour: either Muse Spark counted more than one body inside it, or its length swung more than twofold across frames. Their size is shown as a range and not scored for risk.
- **Alert level** is the highest level the risk engine assigns. Docile species are capped at Warning, and a shark more than 1 km offshore with nobody within 100 m is logged as an Advisory.
- **Lengths** use each clip's ground scale, which comes from a reference object or an assumed altitude (see each manifest), so they are about ±20%.
